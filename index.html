About

I am currently a student with a background in computer science. Before continuing my studies, I worked as a graphic designer and also took on part-time roles in web development and UI/UX design. Through these experiences, I developed a strong interest in the technical side of digital creation and began exploring software development more deeply.

I enjoy coding and continuously learning new technologies, particularly in game and simulation development. Recently, I have been actively exploring Unity, where I am interested in building interactive applications and simulations that combine design, usability, and technical functionality.
